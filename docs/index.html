Privacy Policy for Market Debunk

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

Market Debunk ("we", "our", or "us") operates automated educational content publishing tools ("Market Debunk Engine" and "Market Debunk Tamil"). This policy explains how we handle data and permissions when interacting with Meta platforms (Facebook Graph API and Instagram Graph API).

1. Permissions & Data Accessed

We only access permissions explicitly authorized by you through Meta OAuth:

  • Facebook Page Permissions: pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement to publish educational financial carousel posts and photos to your selected Page.
  • Instagram Graph API Permissions: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish to upload carousel container items and publish scheduled carousels.

We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal data belonging to your followers or page visitors.

2. Purpose of Use

The permissions are strictly used to automate the delivery of educational market infographics, carousel slides, and financial literacy summaries. We do not engage in targeted advertising, user profiling, or third-party data broker sharing.

3. Security & Storage

All API keys and tokens are stored securely in encrypted CI/CD environments. Images generated during the rendering phase are temporarily stored to provide public URLs required by Meta Graph API during carousel container creation.

4. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can revoke access to this app at any time via Facebook:

  1. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Click Apps and Websites or Business Integrations.
  3. Find Market Debunk and click Remove.
