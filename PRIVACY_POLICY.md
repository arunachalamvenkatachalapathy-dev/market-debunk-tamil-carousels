# Privacy Policy for Market Debunk

**Last Updated**: September 5, 2026

**Market Debunk** ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our automation service ("Market Debunk Engine" and "Market Debunk Tamil") interacts with your information when connecting to Meta platforms (Instagram Graph API and Facebook Graph API).

---

## 1. Information We Access
Our application functions as an automated educational content creation and publishing tool. When you grant permissions through Meta (Facebook / Instagram), we access only the technical permissions necessary to publish carousel slides and captions:
- **Facebook Page Metadata & Publishing Permissions**: (`pages_manage_posts`, `pages_read_engagement`) to upload carousel slide photos and publish feed posts to your designated Facebook Page.
- **Instagram Graph API Permissions**: (`instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`) to upload carousel item containers and publish scheduled carousels.

We **do not** collect, store, or sell personal identifiable information (PII) of your page visitors or followers.

---

## 2. How We Use Information
The permissions and data accessed via the Meta Graph API are strictly used for:
- Publishing daily educational financial carousels and infographics to your connected Facebook Page and Instagram profile.
- Retrieving the public permalink of published posts to monitor successful publication.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or data brokering.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security
- Access tokens and API credentials are stored securely in encrypted CI/CD secrets (such as GitHub Actions Secrets) and are never exposed publicly.
- Generated slide images are stored temporarily in your repository to provide publicly accessible raw URLs required by Meta Graph API during carousel container creation.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
Our service interfaces with:
- **Meta Platforms (Instagram & Facebook)**: Subject to Meta's Data Policy.
- **Google Gemini API**: Used strictly to script financial educational summaries from public market news.

---

## 5. User Data Deletion & Revocation
You may revoke our application's access at any time:
1. Open your Facebook account and navigate to **Settings & Privacy -> Settings**.
2. Click **Business Integrations** or **Apps and Websites**.
3. Locate **Market Debunk** and click **Remove**.
